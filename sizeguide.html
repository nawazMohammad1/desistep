Your size, your style.

Every beautiful outfit deserves the right fit. Use our simple measurement guide to discover the size that feels as beautiful as it looks.

The Vastraa Fit

Designed to fit beautifully.

Measurements can vary slightly between styles and silhouettes. Use the guides below as a reference and contact us on WhatsApp if you need help choosing your size.

01

Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your bust. Keep the measuring tape comfortable and parallel to the floor.

02

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline without pulling the tape too tightly.

03

Hips

Measure around the fullest part of your hips while standing naturally.

Easy Measurements

How to measure yourself.

Stand Naturally

Stand straight and relaxed while taking your measurements. Avoid holding your breath or pulling your body in.

Use a Soft Tape

Use a flexible measuring tape and keep it comfortably close to your body.

Wear Light Clothing

Take measurements over light clothing for a more accurate result.

Ask for Help

If possible, ask someone to help you measure for a more accurate fit.

Between two sizes?

If your measurements fall between two sizes, we recommend choosing the size that gives you the comfort you prefer. For custom sizing or product-specific measurements, message our team directly on WhatsApp.

Need Help?

Your perfect fit is only a message away.

Not sure which size to choose? Send us your measurements and our team can help you select the right size for your Vastraa look.
