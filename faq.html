Ordering & WhatsApp Shopping

Ordering on Vastraa is simple and personal. When you find an outfit you love, click the "Order on WhatsApp" button on any product card or page. This opens a direct WhatsApp chat pre-filled with the item name, price, and your selected size. Our team will then confirm availability, share payment details, and process your order.

02

Shipping & Delivery

Standard orders are dispatched within 24 to 48 hours. Domestic delivery typically takes 3–6 business days depending on your location. Express delivery options are available upon request via WhatsApp.

Yes, we ship worldwide! International shipping rates and delivery timelines vary by destination. Reach out to us on WhatsApp with your country and postal code for precise shipping quotes.

03

Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love your Vastraa piece. We offer easy exchanges or returns within 7 days of delivery for unworn items in their original condition with all tags intact. Custom-stitched items are final sale.

Simply drop us a message on WhatsApp with your order number and reason for exchange. Our team will arrange a hassle-free pickup and guide you through selecting your preferred replacement size or item.
