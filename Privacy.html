At Vastraa, we value the trust you place in us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when you browse our website or communicate with us.

By using the Vastraa website, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

01. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Vastraa, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide to us.

Information you provide

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Shipping or delivery details
  • Product preferences and order details
  • Messages or information you send to us

Information collected automatically

When you browse our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, such as your browser type, device type, approximate location, pages visited, and general website usage information.

02. How We Use Your Information

We may use information we receive for purposes such as:

  • Responding to your questions and enquiries.
  • Processing and managing orders.
  • Communicating about products and services.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Improving our website and shopping experience.
  • Understanding general website usage.
  • Preventing misuse, fraud, or unauthorized activity.

03. WhatsApp Orders

Vastraa may use WhatsApp as a convenient channel for customers to enquire about products and place orders.

When you contact us through WhatsApp, information you voluntarily share with us may include your name, phone number, delivery details, product selections, measurements, and other information necessary to respond to your request.

Important: WhatsApp is a third-party communication service. Information exchanged through WhatsApp may also be subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy practices.

04. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Vastraa may use cookies or similar technologies to help the website function properly and understand how visitors interact with our pages.

Cookies may help remember preferences, improve website performance, and provide basic usage information.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not work as expected when cookies are disabled.

05. Information Sharing

Vastraa does not intend to sell your personal information.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to operate the website, process an order, provide a requested service, communicate with you, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

For example, information may be provided to service providers involved in website hosting, delivery, payment processing, communication, or technical support, where applicable.

06. Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07. Your Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

You may contact Vastraa to ask questions about information you have provided to us or to request appropriate changes where applicable.

You can also choose not to provide certain information, although this may prevent us from providing some services or completing an order.

08. Children's Privacy

Vastraa's website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children without appropriate consent.

09. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, social media platforms, payment services, or other external services.

Vastraa is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before providing personal information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, website, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact Vastraa

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us.
